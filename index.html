Course Glossary

Delegation Loop[cite: 1]
The six-step rhythm of giving AI a job: define, delegate, observe, intervene, verify, accept.[cite: 1]
Delegation Brief[cite: 1]
Six questions answered before AI starts: outcome, context, constraints, authority, deliverable, verification.[cite: 1]
Outcome[cite: 1]
What should exist when the work is complete.[cite: 1]
Context[cite: 1]
What AI needs to know that it cannot know on its own, including sources provided.[cite: 1]
Constraints[cite: 1]
The explicit requirements and limits that apply to the job.[cite: 1]
Authority[cite: 1]
What AI may decide without asking vs. what it must seek permission for.[cite: 1]
Deliverable[cite: 1]
The concrete form the result takes (table, file, doc) shaped so someone can use it.[cite: 1]
Verification[cite: 1]
What you will check before accepting, defined prior to starting the run.[cite: 1]
Plan-First[cite: 1]
Asking AI to list its planned steps and wait for explicit go-ahead before starting.[cite: 1]
Project & Knowledge[cite: 1]
A container in an assistant holding persistent files (knowledge) and standing rules across chats.[cite: 1]
Temporary/Incognito Chat[cite: 1]
A session that is not saved to history and does not feed into persistent AI memory.[cite: 1]
Delegation Record[cite: 1]
The single-page audit record tracking brief, run details, verification checks, and final human decision.[cite: 1]
